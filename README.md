### 5. 🏥 HelixMed AI — Clinical Intelligence & Patient Digital Twin Platform

🩺 **Patient Digital Twin:** Visualizes longitudinal health records and simulated 180-day patient trajectory forecasts.

💊 **Clinical Safety:** Supports medication safety audits, physiological early-warning alerts, and human-reviewed SOAP note generation.

🏥 **Hospital Operations:** Includes surgery scheduling, billing intelligence, blood bank management, infection surveillance, staff rosters, ambulance tracking, and medical asset management.

🧬 **Advanced Clinical Tools:** Features genomic variant exploration, a DICOM viewer, ICU/EEG telemetry, clinical trial matching, protein docking visualization, and an HL7 FHIR R4 pipeline.

🤖 **Clinical Knowledge RAG:** Retrieves clinical guideline information to support knowledge-grounded workflows.

🚀 **Deployment:** Supports Docker-based deployment, container images, and Kubernetes deployment configurations.

**🛠️ Tech Stack:** `Python` · `React` · `PyTorch` · `XGBoost` · `LightGBM` · `Scikit-learn` · `Docker` · `Kubernetes` · `GitHub Actions`
